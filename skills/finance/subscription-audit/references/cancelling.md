# Cancelling one subscription

Do these in order for each item the owner named.

1. **Quote the approval.** Write down the owner's words and today's date. If
   they named a bundle or a plan with several seats, confirm which part.
2. **Look at the live account.** It may already be cancelled, paused, or on a
   different plan than the list says. If it is already ending, change nothing
   and record the end date.
3. **Find where it is billed.** A plan bought through Apple, Google, a phone
   carrier or a reseller can be cancelled only there. The receipt names the
   biller.
4. **Check what depends on it.** Data to export, a replacement that must work
   first, other people who use it, sign-ins that go through it. If something
   is not ready, stop and tell the owner what is missing.
5. **Get in without changing the account.** Try in this order: the saved
   login, an emailed sign-in code, then a browser the owner is already signed
   in to, if they offer one. Do not reset the password to get in.
6. **Make the smallest change that stops the charge.** Try the account's
   billing page first, then the manage or cancel link in the latest receipt
   email. Where the plan has no cancel button, turn off auto-renew. Do not
   delete the account, the data, or the payment method. Refuse offers, pauses
   and downgrades unless the owner asked for one.
7. **Copy the proof.** Try in this order and keep all you get: the
   confirmation screen's exact words with the date access ends and the final
   amount due, the confirmation email, then the billing page's status after a
   reload.
8. **Clean up.** Close the browser session you opened and leave the owner's
   screen as you found it.

## When you are blocked

A site may ask for a code on the owner's phone, block automated browsers, or
force a phone call or chat to cancel. Do not work around it. Tell the owner:

- the page to open,
- each click, in order, with the button's exact label,
- what the final screen should say,
- to send a screenshot of that screen.

Record the cancel only after you have read the screenshot.

## After

Note the date of the first charge that should not happen. If that charge
appears, tell the owner the same day with the statement line, and say that a
dispute with the card issuer is their choice to make.
