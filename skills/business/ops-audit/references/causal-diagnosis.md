# Causal diagnosis

For each observed sink, write the actual path: trigger, collection, judgment, action, destination, read-back, failure visibility, and existing coverage. Make separate cards when paths differ. A card carries the fields `scripts/validator.py` requires, with cost or unknown, evidence, gaps, and the named approval among them; `fixtures/roadmap.json` shows a complete one.

Choose in this order. **Keep human** when the work is the owner's craft or their people: writing that goes out under their name, and community. **Remove** when nothing depends on it. **Define or simplify** when the process is unclear; what is not defined cannot be optimised, and automating it only makes the mess faster. **Deterministic rule or plain script** when the same job needs no model. **Leverage move** when one act by the agent settles it. **Agent automation** last, bounded to one fixed task, and only for work the owner has already done the same way more than once: never automate an unproven workflow. An agent receives a fixed task and output shape, cannot select the next step, and sits between deterministic collection and a gate. Name the approver for consequential actions. Mark absent proof as unknown, not likely.

`proof` states what would show the action worked, in audit words. It is not the evidence for the diagnosis; that is `evidence`. The plan's done-when line says the same thing in the owner's words.

The verdict sets the step kind. `leverage move` becomes a move the agent performs once under approval. `automate with an agent` becomes an automation. `keep human` becomes a hold with a stated end, except craft and community, whose hold has no end.
